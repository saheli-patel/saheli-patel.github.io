# Baked Pasta with Vegetables

## Ingredients
- 1 pound (450g) dried pasta like rigatoni or ziti
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 medium onion, about 6 ounces
- 2 small bell peppers, about 8 ounces
- 8 ounces mushrooms, 1 ¼ cups sliced
- 1/2 teaspoon fennel seeds or ground fennel
- 3 cups homemade pasta sauce or 1 (28oz) jar of store-bought sauce
- Handful of fresh basil leaves, torn or coarsely chopped
- 6 tablespoons (90g) ricotta cheese
- 2 cups (226g) shredded mozzarella cheese
- Salt and fresh ground black pepper

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). If you have an oven-safe deep skillet, use it to cook the veggies and bake the pasta. Otherwise, lightly grease a 3-quart baking dish with olive oil.
2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta. Cook it two minutes less than the recommended time on the package (it will finish cooking in the oven). Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining.
3. Slice the onions and bell peppers into 1/4-inch thick slices. Clean the mushrooms with a damp paper towel, remove hard stems, and slice into 1/4-inch pieces.
4. Heat a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Add olive oil, then mushrooms in a single layer. Cook until browned on one side (3–5 minutes). Stir, then add onions and peppers. Cook for another 3–5 minutes until softened and slightly browned.
5. Season the vegetables with salt and black pepper (about 1/4 teaspoon each). Stir in fennel, pasta sauce, and basil.
6. Toss the cooked pasta with the vegetable mixture. If the skillet is too small, combine the pasta and sauce in a large bowl. Add a splash of reserved pasta water if the mixture seems dry.
7. Taste and adjust seasoning with more salt and pepper. Transfer the pasta mixture to the baking dish if not using an oven-safe skillet.
8. Tuck spoonfuls of ricotta cheese around the pasta and vegetables. Top with shredded mozzarella cheese.
9. Bake the pasta, uncovered, for 20–25 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbling.
10. Serve hot and enjoy your baked pasta with vegetables!
