# Dahi Ke Aloo (Potatoes in Buttermilk)

## Ingredients
- 5 medium-sized boiled potatoes
- 300 gm curd
- 3 cups water
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1/4 tsp jeera (cumin seeds)
- 1/4 tsp rai (mustard seeds)
- 1/8 tsp asafoetida (hing)
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tbsp + 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 2 green chillies, chopped
- 3 tbsp water
- Salt, as per taste
- 1 tsp garam masala powder
- 1 tsp oil (for tempering)
- 2 dry red chillies
- Chopped green coriander leaves

## Instructions
1. Wash, boil, peel, and mash the potatoes into medium chunks. Set them aside in a bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, churn the curd nicely. Add 3 cups of water and churn again to prepare buttermilk (chach).
3. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok. Splutter rai, jeera, and asafoetida (hing). Add red chilli powder, coriander powder, turmeric powder, and chopped green chillies, followed by 3 tbsp water. Cook the masala on low heat for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally to avoid burning.
4. Once the masala is cooked, take out 1 tbsp of it and set it aside for later use.
5. Add the buttermilk to the wok. Increase the heat to high and stir continuously until it starts boiling. Quick stirring is essential to prevent the buttermilk from curdling.
6. When the buttermilk starts boiling, add the mashed potatoes and salt. Mix well.
7. Reduce the heat to low and let the curry simmer for 30 minutes. (Alternatively, cook on medium heat for 15 minutes, but slow cooking enhances the flavor.)
8. Turn off the heat and add garam masala powder and chopped green coriander leaves.
9. For tempering, heat 1 tsp oil in a small pan. Roast the dry red chillies for half a minute, then add the reserved masala. Pour this tempering over the curry just before serving.
10. Serve hot and enjoy your flavorful Dahi Ke Aloo with rice or roti!
