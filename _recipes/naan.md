# Butter Naan

## Ingredients
- 2 cups (250 grams) all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons sugar
- 1 to 1½ teaspoons grated garlic (optional)
- 2 tablespoons oil or melted butter
- ½ cup yogurt
- ¼ cup + 2 tablespoons lukewarm milk (adjust as needed)
- Extra flour for dusting
- 3 tablespoons melted butter (for brushing)
- 5 to 6 garlic cloves, chopped (optional)
- ¼ cup coriander leaves, chopped (optional)
- Kalonji seeds (nigella seeds) (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, mix flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, sugar, and garlic (if using).
2. Add oil or butter, yogurt, and lukewarm milk. Knead into a soft, pliable dough.
3. Cover and rest the dough for 30 minutes to 1 hour.
4. Divide into 6 portions, roll into balls.
5. Lightly flour your surface. Roll each ball into an oval or tear shape.
6. Optionally sprinkle garlic, coriander, or kalonji on top. Press in gently with a rolling pin.

### Cooking (Option 1: Skillet)
7. Heat a skillet on medium-high. Place naan on it.
8. Cook until bubbles form and golden spots appear. Flip and repeat.
9. Remove and brush with melted butter.

### Cooking (Option 2: Wire Rack Over Flame)
10. Place naan over open flame using a wire rack.
11. Cook both sides until charred spots appear.
12. Brush with butter and serve hot.

## Serving Suggestions
Serve hot with paneer butter masala, dal makhani, or butter chicken.
